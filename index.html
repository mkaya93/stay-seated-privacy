Stay Seated

Privacy Policy

Stay Seated: Watch Order · Effective September 29, 2026

Stay Seated doesn't collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • What you've watched, Codex files you've read, quiz answers, achievements, streaks, spoiler reveals and settings.
  • Your watch timer and the predictions you make during a watch.
  • A small progress summary shared with the app's own widgets, Live Activity and Control Center control, through storage that only Stay Seated can access on your device.

None of this is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app downloads

  • Content (titles, watch order, Codex files, where-to-watch info) is read from our content server, hosted by Supabase. These requests don't include any account, identifier or your progress. Like any web request, they carry standard technical information such as your IP address, which the host may log for security and operations.
  • Posters and images load from Wikimedia (Wikipedia's media servers).
  • The app uses your device's region setting only on the device, to choose which streaming services to show.

Links to other services

"Watch on…", trailer and store buttons open other apps and websites (for example Disney+, Apple TV or YouTube). What you do there is covered by their privacy policies, not this one.

Notifications, Live Activities and Siri

  • Watch-time polls, post-credit reminders and case-file nudges are local notifications scheduled on your device. They're optional, and you can turn them off in Settings.
  • Live Activities, widgets and Siri / Shortcuts actions run on your device. Siri requests are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Beta testing

If you test Stay Seated through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to send with us, under Apple's privacy policy. We use them only to fix and improve the app.

Children

Stay Seated doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Questions? Open an issue, or use "Send Beta Feedback" in TestFlight.

Stay Seated is a free, unofficial fan app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Marvel Studios or Disney.
